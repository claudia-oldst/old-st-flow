# Portal Billing

**Tab:** Billing on `/h/:hash` and in the Client Portal Editor preview.

Per-epic table: Total Original (expandable into Original Std/Proj, Change Requests, Bugs), Latest Revised Estimate, one column per selected month of logged hours (UK time), and Actual Total across the whole project. Footer shows totals in hours and in GBP at the project rate (hidden when rate is 0).

- Month filter defaults to the latest 5 months up to the cutoff.
- Scoped to the portal's versions and cutoff. Computed live from tickets and time logs — nothing is stored.
